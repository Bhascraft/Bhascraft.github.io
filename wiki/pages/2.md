# Survival

**Survival**, the main gamemode, is a (mostly) rules-free open world. After entering the *Survival Entrance* portal in the Hub, you will arrive at spawn.

<div class="callout callout-info">
<strong>This is the post-reset map</strong>
<p>The previous Survival world was lost in the data-loss incident on 31 May 2026 and rebuilt from scratch. Rather than generating new chunks on the fly as players explore — which is a common source of server lag — the new map was <strong>pregenerated</strong> ahead of time using the datapacks below. ## World Generation

The whole map — Overworld, [Nether](#nether), and [End](#end) — was pregenerated for this reset using a stack of datapacks on top of vanilla generation. See the [Overworld](#overworld), [Nether](#nether), and [End](#end) pages for what's active in each dimension and screenshots of the results.

The spawn area is a 3×3 un-removable platform stretching down to bedrock. There is also a 3×3×3 protected cube around the spawn location where you can't break or place blocks, to prevent instant trapping.

## Rules

| Rule | Status |
|------|--------|
| Griefing, stealing, and PVP | ✔ Allowed |
| X-ray (anti-X-ray is active, reducing its effectiveness) | ✔ Allowed |
| Hack clients, mods, and AFK spoofers | ✘ Not Allowed |

## Commands

Here are some survival-related commands (all of them are also listed on the Commands page):

- `/warp Survival` — teleports you to survival (same as entering the portal)
- `/msg` — private messaging *(Master rank)*
- `/trash` — opens a disposal chest to destroy items *(Veteran rank)*

## Extra Info

- All normal vanilla features are functional.
- There is no economy system.
- Survival has a warp — use `/warp Survival` to get there instantly.
