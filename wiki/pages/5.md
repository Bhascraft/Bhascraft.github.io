# End

The End was regenerated along with the rest of the map in the [31 May 2026 reset](#bhascraft-docs) and pregenerated ahead of time. Note that [Trek](#overworld) also reworks the vanilla End City structure specifically — Nullscape below is about the terrain around it.

## Generation

| Datapack | What it adds |
|---|---|
| Nullscape | Replaces the vanilla flat End islands with rolling terrain and tall pale spires ("End Reborn"). |

**Its an end update!!**
