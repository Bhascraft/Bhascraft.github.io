# Nether

The Nether was regenerated along with the rest of the map in the [31 May 2026 reset](#bhascraft-docs) and pregenerated ahead of time.

## Generation

| Datapack | What it adds |
|---|---|
| Amplified Nether | Much taller, more dramatic Nether terrain than vanilla generates. |
| Amplified Nether Height | Extends the Nether's build height to match Amplified Nether's taller terrain. |

Vanilla Nether structures — bastions, fortresses, and the rest — still generate as normal on top of the amplified terrain as well as many custom variants!.
