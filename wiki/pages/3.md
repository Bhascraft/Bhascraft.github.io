# Overworld

The main Survival world (see the [Survival](#survival) page for rules and commands). Like the rest of the map, it was regenerated from scratch as part of the [31 May 2026 reset](#bhascraft-docs) and pregenerated ahead of time to avoid on-the-fly generation lag.

## Generation

On top of vanilla terrain, the following packs are active:

| Datapack | What it adds |
|---|---|
| Terralith | Overhauls overworld terrain and biome variety ("Overworld Evolved"). |
| Structory | Adds additional structures. |
| Explorify | Adds more structures to find while exploring. |
| Dungeons and Taverns | Adds unique new structures in the vanilla style. |
| Towns and Towers (T&T) | Overhauls village and outpost structures and their loot. |
| Trek | Replaces several vanilla structures — Pillager Outposts, Strongholds, Woodland Mansions, and more — with new designs. |
