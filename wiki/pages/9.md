## PVP (Removed)

### Location

Enter the Nether-type portal to your left in the Hub to be teleported to the PVP arena. It is FFA-type PVP in a limited area, with items obtained from kits in the safe spawn zone.

### Arena

The arena is approximately **90 × 80 × 103 blocks** (a rough circle).

![PVP arena overview](images/image_1781432457851_0.webp)

It has some texture and a small cave on the surface to add interest. Block breaking and placing were disabled.

When you walked out of the safe spawn area into the arena, you would receive a *"You Entered The Arena"* message. Once inside, the only ways to leave were to warp out with `/warp` or to die.

### Kits

To the right of spawn were some spout/pipe structures that spew particles — standing under one would give you the corresponding kit.

![PVP kit dispensers](images/image_1781432386235_0.webp)

Only the **Mace** kit was ever released before removal. Sword and Crystal kits were planned but never added.

## SkyBlock (Removed)

### Getting There

You used to get to SkyBlock by punching the NPC in the Hub, or running `/island` (or `/is`).

![Skyblock NPC in Hub](images/image_1781434482313_0.webp)

### How It Works

SkyBlock was the classic skyblock experience — you started on an island of your choice and built outwards, gathering materials as you went.

When you first started, you'd see a chest UI showing the four islands you could choose from:

![Island choice 1](images/image_1781435105028_0.png)
![Island choice 2](images/image_1781435138135_0.png)
![Island choice 3](images/image_1781435166845_0.png)
![Island choice 4](images/image_1781435184881_0.png)

Once your island was created, you'd be teleported to it to begin.

### Commands

> These commands no longer work — kept for reference only.

#### Island Management

| Command | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| `/island` | Displayed the main island help menu or interface. |
| `/island go` | Teleported you straight to your island home. |
| `/island sethome [name]` | Set your island home teleport point at your current position. |
| `/island homes` | Listed all island home teleport locations you had set. |
| `/island deletehome [name]` | Deleted a specific home teleport point. |
| `/island reset` | Deleted your current island entirely so you could start fresh. |
| `/island info` | Displayed detailed information about your island or another player's island. |
| `/island near` | Showed the names of neighbouring islands around your location. |
| `/island spawn` | Teleported you back to the SkyBlock world's spawn point. |

#### Team Management

| Command | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| `/island team` | Opened the team management menu. |
| `/island team invite [player]` | Invited a player to join your island team. |
| `/island team accept` | Accepted a pending invitation to join an island team. |
| `/island team reject` | Rejected a pending island team invitation. |
| `/island team leave` | Left your current island team. |
| `/island team kick [player]` | Removed a member from your island team. |
| `/island team trust [player]` | Gave a player permanent building access without adding them to the team. |
| `/island team untrust [player]` | Revoked permanent trusted status from a player. |
| `/island team coop [player]` | Gave an online player temporary building access to your island. |
| `/island team uncoop [player]` | Revoked temporary co-op status from a player. |
| `/island team promote [player]` | Promoted a team member up a rank on your island. |
| `/island team demote [player]` | Demoted a team member down a rank on your island. |
| `/island team setowner [player]` | Transferred ownership of the island to another team member. |

#### Social & Security

| Command | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| `/island ban [player]` | Banned a player from entering your island. |
| `/island unban [player]` | Lifted an island ban, allowing the player to visit again. |
| `/island banlist` | Listed all players currently banned from your island. |
