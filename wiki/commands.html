Commands

All commands available to players on Bhascraft. Commands marked with a rank require that rank or higher.

General Commands

Available to every player regardless of rank:

/playtime
Displays your total playtime on the server.
/warp [name]
Teleports you to a public server warp location.
/warp list
Lists all available public warp destinations.

Creative Plot Commands

💡 TipYou can use /p as a shortcut for /plot.
/plot auto
Automatically finds and claims the nearest empty plot.
/plot claim
Claims the plot you are currently standing on.
/plot home
Teleports you to your plot.
/plot clear
Resets all blocks on your plot to the default flat state.
/plot delete
Clears and unclaims your plot entirely.
/plot list
Lists plots based on specified criteria.
/plot visit [player]
Teleports you to another player's plot.
/plot add [player]
Allows a player to build on your plot while you are online.
/plot trust [player]
Gives a player permanent build access to your plot.
/plot remove [player]
Revokes build access from an added or trusted player.
/plot confirm
Confirms a destructive action such as clearing or deleting.

WorldEdit Commands

WorldEdit is available in the Creative world only.

//wand
Gives you the region selection tool (usually a wooden axe).
//pos1 / //pos2
Sets selection position 1 or 2 at your current eye location.
//hpos1 / //hpos2
Sets selection position 1 or 2 at the block you are looking at.
//set [block]
Fills your entire selection with a specific block.
//replace [from] [to]
Replaces one block type with another inside your selection.
//walls [block]
Builds a perimeter wall around the edges of your selection.
//faces [block]
Creates a hollow box (walls, floor, and ceiling) around your selection.
//copy
Copies your selection to your clipboard.
//cut
Removes your selection and copies it to your clipboard.
//flip
Flips your clipboard selection directionally.
//rotate [angle]
Rotates your clipboard selection by a given number of degrees.
//paste
Pastes your clipboard relative to your current position.
//undo
Reverts your last WorldEdit action.
//redo
Re-applies your last undone WorldEdit action.

SkyBlock Commands

💡 TipYou can use /is as a shortcut for /island.
/island
Opens the main SkyBlock interface menu.
/island create
Generates a new starter island for you.
/island go / /island home
Teleports you straight back to your island home.
/island sethome
Sets your primary island teleport destination.
/island homes
Displays all home locations you have set on your island.
/island deletehome [name]
Deletes a specific island home point.
/island reset
Deletes your current island so you can start over.
/island info
Shows technical data about your island.
/island language
Opens the language settings menu.
/island name [name]
Sets a custom display name for your island.
/island near
Lists nearby islands around your location.
/island spawn
Warps you to the SkyBlock world's spawn.
/island settings
Opens a GUI to toggle island rules and permissions.
/island expel [player]
Forces a visitor out of your island.
/island ban [player]
Bans a player from entering your island.
/island team
Opens the team configuration menu.
/island team invite [player]
Invites a player to your island team.
/island team accept / reject
Accepts or rejects an island team invitation.
/island team leave
Leaves your current island team.
/island team kick [player]
Removes a member from your island team.
/island team trust [player]
Gives permanent build access without joining the team.
/island team coop [player]
Gives an online player temporary build access.
/island team promote/demote [player]
Promotes or demotes a team member.
/island team setowner [player]
Transfers island ownership to another member.

Rank-Locked Commands

Master Rank

/msg [player] [message]
Sends a private direct message to another online player.

Veteran Rank

/disposal
Opens a temporary trash UI where you can destroy items permanently.

Champion Rank

/skull
Spawns a decorative player head.
/skull [player]
Spawns a skull with a specified player's skin.

Legend Rank

/nick [nickname]
Sets a custom chat display nickname.
/nick [color/format]
Applies MiniMessage formatting or colour to your nickname.
/pp or /playerparticles
Opens the particle effects menu to apply cosmetic effects.
