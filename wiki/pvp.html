PVP

Location

Enter the Nether-type portal to your left in the Hub to be teleported to the PVP arena. It is FFA-type PVP in a limited area, with items obtained from kits in the safe spawn zone.

Arena

The arena is approximately 90 × 80 × 103 blocks (a rough circle).

It has some texture and a small cave on the surface to add interest. Block breaking and placing are currently disabled.

When you walk out of the safe spawn area into the arena, you will receive a "You Entered The Arena" message. Once inside, the only ways to leave are to warp out with /warp or to die.

Kits

To the right of spawn are some spout/pipe structures that spew particles — standing under one will give you the corresponding kit.

Currently only the Mace kit is available. Sword and Crystal kits will be added in the future, and block-decay mechanics still need to be balanced.
